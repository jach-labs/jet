Still not in the open

Everything above approximates the interface (state + typed options → one-pass probabilities). None of it has these:

  • TypeSafe's Jev weights. Every "openjev" / "-RLCD" Hub repo is either a stock model plus decoding code, or an independently trained head.
  • The RLCD algorithm, loss or data. Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions is described only at blog-post level; no public implementation.
  • A trained open model matching Jev's calibration claims. The best open scorers now reach ~90% agreement with Jev (Bespoke Nimble; snapjudge measures 81.2% for Qwen3.8-27B against Jev's own 87.7%), but they still softmax over candidate logits and the studies find confidence does not reliably flag errors.
  • Archer Hume's promised open-weight version. Announced with the architecture post; training "takes time". A release from his company, Solomon, appeared 2026-09-21; its launch post calls it the best open-weight alternative to Jev, though the model card documents a document-QA model and never uses the word. Listed as a delivery of the promise, not as Jev reconstructed.
